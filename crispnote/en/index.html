CrispNote

by Craftopia

A privacy-conscious note app where checklists and notes stay laid out side by side

CrispNote is a Keep-style app that lets you manage checklist-style notes and freeform notes together in one grid. Your data is stored directly in your own Google Drive or OneDrive account — it never passes through any server the developer runs.

Key features

  • Checklists (memos) and freeform notes shown together in one grid
  • Backup and multi-device sync via Google Drive or OneDrive (both optional — you can skip cloud sync entirely)
  • Image and file attachments
  • PIN and biometric lock, for the whole app or individual notes
  • No ads, no account required (signing in with Google/Microsoft is only needed for cloud sync)

Web version
