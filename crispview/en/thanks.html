Issuing your key. This takes a few seconds...

The same key is also emailed to you. It can end up in the spam folder, so it is safest to copy it from here.

How to enter it

  1. Open CrispView and choose Purchase from the ⋮ menu
  2. Paste the key into "Already have a licence key?"
  3. When it changes to "Purchased", you are done - the ads stop showing

If you are signed in to OneDrive, your other devices pick this up automatically. The licence is shared through your own OneDrive app folder, so entering it on one device is enough; the rest catch up at their next refresh.

If it does not arrive

  • Please check your spam folder first. The mail is sent by an individual developer and is sometimes filtered
  • If nothing arrives after a few minutes, contact anesapp.support@gmail.com. Tell us the name or email address you used and we will look it up right away

Your receipt is sent automatically by Paddle, who handle the payment (separately from the licence key email).
