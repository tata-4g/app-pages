CrispView

by Craftopia

Browse your OneDrive files by date, the way a photo app does

CrispView shows the files in your OneDrive as a date-by-date grid of thumbnails. Not just photos and videos - PDFs, Word, Excel and Markdown share the same timeline. The whole point is that you can find things by when they are from, not by where you happened to put them.

What it does

  • Photos, videos and documents on one timeline. This is not a gallery that only picks up images
  • Hide the folders you do not want to see. Files that other apps leave behind, or working folders, can be excluded by folder or by file
  • Browsing, search, tags and albums all run on your device. Once loaded, the timeline works offline and scrolling never waits on the network
  • Tags, albums, pins and date corrections are stored in your own OneDrive app folder, so they line up across your devices. Nothing passes through a server of ours
  • Search inside files as well as by name (PDF, Office, plain text)
  • Rename, move, delete, share links, upload - and restore from the recycle bin
  • Folders on the device itself can be browsed too, as a separate view that never mixes with the OneDrive index
  • A home screen widget (Android) and offline copies
